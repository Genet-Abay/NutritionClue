About NutritionClue

Supporting women's health through nutrition during life's important transitions

Our Mission

At NutritionClue, we believe that every woman deserves access to nutritional guidance that supports her through the unique challenges of pre-menopause and menopause. Our mission is to empower women with the knowledge, tools, and community support needed to thrive during this important phase of life.

What We Offer

👩‍🍳 Cooking Lessons

Expert-led cooking tutorials that teach you how to prepare hormone-friendly meals in your own kitchen.

📖 Recipe Collection

A curated collection of recipes with detailed ingredients and nutritional benefits tailored for women's health.

🏃‍♀️ Physical Activity Guide

Exercise recommendations designed for menopause, focusing on bone health, flexibility, and overall fitness.

💬 Community Support

A welcoming community where women can share experiences, ask questions, and support each other.

Our Approach

We take a holistic, science-based approach to women's nutrition. Our recommendations are:

  • Evidence-Based: Grounded in nutritional science and research on women's health
  • Personalized: Tailored to individual needs, preferences, and health goals
  • Practical: Easy to implement in your daily life
  • Supportive: Backed by a community of women on similar journeys

Why Menopause Nutrition Matters

During pre-menopause and menopause, a woman's nutritional needs change significantly. Proper nutrition can help:

  • Manage hot flashes and night sweats
  • Maintain bone density and prevent osteoporosis
  • Support heart health
  • Manage weight changes
  • Improve mood and reduce anxiety
  • Boost energy levels
  • Support better sleep
