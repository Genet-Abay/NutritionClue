Exercise Tips for Menopause

🌡️ Stay Cool

Exercise in cooler environments to manage hot flashes. Consider morning workouts or air-conditioned spaces.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water before, during, and after exercise to stay hydrated and help regulate body temperature.

🦴 Focus on Bones

Include weight-bearing exercises to maintain bone density and reduce the risk of osteoporosis.

🧘 Mind-Body Connection

Practice yoga or tai chi to reduce stress, improve balance, and support mental well-being.

👂 Listen to Your Body

Adjust intensity based on how you feel. Some days may be more challenging than others.

🔄 Stay Consistent

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week. Consistency is more important than intensity.

Getting Started

Before beginning any new exercise routine, consider these steps:

  1. Consult with your healthcare provider, especially if you have any health concerns
  2. Start slowly and gradually increase intensity over time
  3. Choose activities you enjoy to stay motivated
  4. Find an exercise buddy for support and accountability
  5. Track your progress and celebrate small wins
